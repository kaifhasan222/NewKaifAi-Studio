## 4. Content ka rule (algorithm ke liye)
Neeche ke points web search se hain (blogs, official nahi), isliye guideline maano, pakka niyam nahi.
- Shorts ko pehle chhote group ko dikhaya jata hai. Agar log jaldi swipe kar dein ya kam dekhein, to aage reach nahi milti.
- **Pehle 2-3 second hook**: sawal ya shock wali line, intro nahi, "Namaste dosto" nahi.
- Short rakho (25-45 sec). Aakhir ka line shuru se jud jaye (loop) to dobara dekhte hain.
- Ek hi topic par channel: tech + AI. Gaming, motivation, comedy mix mat karo (purane channel mein yehi bikhra tha).
- Purane video dobara upload mat karo. Original content hi push hota hai.
- Pehle 1 ghante mein comment ka jawab do, pinned comment mein sawal poocho.
- Roz ek upload, ek hi time (shaam 6-8 baje try karo), 30 din tak. Analytics se best time dekhna.
- Misleading title mat do. Title ke wada ko video pura kare.
