# YouTube Shorts in India: top niches, channels, formats, and Oct-Nov 2026 trends

Research date: 2026-11-08. Every claim has a source. Older data is labelled. Blog and listicle figures are flagged as unverified.

## Q1. Which Shorts categories/niches get the most views in India in 2026?

### Takeaway
I found no India-specific 2026 ranking of Shorts categories. The views data points to three leaders: (a) non-verbal or light-dialogue family/comedy skits, (b) relatable desi comedy and daily-life mini-vlogs, and (c) kids/family "Tom & Jerry-style" content. Behind these come DIY/craft/hacks, food, gaming (GTA/Minecraft, Free Fire, BGMI), festival content, and AI-generated character channels. Pure tech/education is a smaller slice, but one tech Shorts channel (Tech Master Shorts) made YouTube's India top-10 creators list for 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Week of 26 Oct to 1 Nov 2026: 21 of the 50 most-viewed channels worldwide were Indian. The Indian Shorts channels on the list were KL BRO Biju Rithvik (#4, 866M weekly views), Happy L story (#15, 609.5M), Koko & Golu (#16, 602.8M), Sukhasan Ka Star (#18, 583M), Mischief Time (#20, 558M), Fit Arshia (#24, 523M), Smiti Choudhury (#30, 476M), Gneef (#36, 425M), Pinki Prajapati (#37, 421M), 7amoda Gaming (#41, 391.5M, gaming) and Misti Happy Lifestyle Games & Shorts (#42, 389M). The Indian channels without the Shorts icon were T-Series (#2, 943M), Anaya Kandhal (#5, 799M), Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah (#10, 670M), Keshav Shashi Vlogs (#11, 661M), Dangal TV (#13, 651M), Adi Connection, Krishna Avyu, Sejal Gaba (#47, 358M) and Apachho Singh. — [Tubefilter, Top 50 week of 11/01/2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/11/02/top-50-most-viewed-youtube-channels-worldwide-week-of-11-01-2026/)
- YouTube's 2025 India report (Dec 2025, older data) points to a rise in "Shorts-first, non-verbal channels" that use music and visual gags instead of dialogue. Its example is KL BRO Biju Rithvik (over 79M subs at that time), which makes "Tom & Jerry-style" family adventures. Korean creator KIMPRO uses non-verbal visual challenges to "gamify" Shorts for Indian viewers. — [Buzzincontent on YouTube India 2025 year-end](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/sejal-gaba-keshav-shashi-raj-shamani-among-youtubes-top-creators-in-india-for-2025-10866634)

These are Kapwing study estimates from late 2025. LatestLY gives about 2.4B views instead. — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/indian-ai-monkey-channel-bandar-apna-dost-pulls-425m-ai-slop-floods-youtube-1766925); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/technology/bandar-apna-dost-leads-the-worlds-highest-earning-ai-slop-channels-as-study-finds-20-of-youtube-recommendations-are-now-ai-generated-content-7256480.html); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/indias-bandar-apna-dost-tops-ai-generated-youtube-channels/tldr)

### Inferences
- The highest-view Indian Shorts are mass-audience and language-light: family and kid skits, relatable comedy, daily-life vlogs. Tech/AI explainer Shorts compete in a smaller pool, but the right packaging can break out (see Tech Master Shorts below).
- AI-generated character stories are a proven high-view format from India. Platform policy risk is rising.

### Gaps

## Q3. What formats work?

### Takeaway
The best-documented signals: language-light or non-verbal storytelling travels across India's languages; dubbing and multi-language versions are now standard; hooks must land in the first 1-5 seconds; and 30-60 seconds is the commonly cited sweet spot. Hindi titles on top trending Shorts are written in Roman-script Hinglish with heavy hashtags.

### Cited Findings
- Non-verbal, music-plus-visual-gag Shorts channels are a named 2025 India trend (KL BRO Biju, KIMPRO). — [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/sejal-gaba-keshav-shashi-raj-shamani-among-youtubes-top-creators-in-india-for-2025-10866634)
- Success in India is "no longer contingent" on region or language, per YouTube 2025. Pan-India dubbing is standard: the Coolie trailer came out in 4 languages and Ashish Chanchlani's Ekaki was dubbed into 5. — [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/sejal-gaba-keshav-shashi-raj-shamani-among-youtubes-top-creators-in-india-for-2025-10866634)
- 77% of Indian Gen Z watched dubbed or translated content (YouTube-linked report, Dec 2025). — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/industry-updates/77gen-z-india-watched-dubbed-content-10892203); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/indian-gen-z-embraces-translated-international-content-as-language-barriers-dissolve-507375-2025-12-19)
- Memes inside scripted stories: Italian Brainrot "Tung tung tung sahur" and Labubu entered India's online vocabulary. Creators Carry Depie, Ayush More and Wanderers Hub used them in scripted stories, horror satire and Minecraft/Roblox worlds. — [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/sejal-gaba-keshav-shashi-raj-shamani-among-youtubes-top-creators-in-india-for-2025-10866634)
- Length, secondhand data: a Vidooly analysis of 2M+ Shorts found 40-60 seconds had the highest average views. An Inflow study of 5,400 Shorts found 50-60 seconds got about 22x more views than under 10 seconds. An older Social Samosa report (2023) said Shorts over 15 seconds get the largest share of viewership. — [Social Samosa 2023](https://www.socialsamosa.com/2023/03/youtube-shorts-longer-than-15-seconds-gets-maximum-share-of-viewership-report/); [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/story/youtube-shorts-between-16-to-60-seconds-duration-garner-maximum-engagement-report)
- Adobe Express creator survey (Mar 2026; opinion, not measured): 53% said under 30 seconds performs best for views. — [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/youtube-shorts-length-study)
- Hooks: vidIQ (Mar 2026) says a 3-5 second hook drives retention. An India vendor guide (no data) recommends a hook within 1 second, 30-45 seconds length, and bold animated subtitles for sound-off viewing. — [Fluxnote India Shorts strategy](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-strategy-india-2026); [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length)
- Observed packaging on India's trending Shorts (Oct-Nov 2026): Roman-script Hinglish titles with emoji and hashtag stacks, for example "Agar aapko Diwali par ghar ki safai karni hai #reels #shorts", "Sabke Pados me ek aise Aunty jaroor hoti hai", "Diwali Ke Patakhe #diwali2026". The usual themes are relatable "har ghar ki kahani" situations. — [Trends24 India Shorts](https://youtube.trends24.in/india/shorts/)

### Inferences
- For a Hinglish tech channel, the documented winners suggest: a visual hook in the first second, on-screen captions, a relatable framing ("har Indian ke phone mein..."), and 30-60 seconds. Searches lean toward "kaise banaye" how-to intent (see Q4).
- Face vs faceless: both win. Face-led examples are Sejal Gaba and Keshav Shashi. Faceless or AI examples are Bandar Apna Dost and Tech Master Shorts, which is mostly screen/phone demos (format inferred, not verified).

### Gaps
- No verified India-specific data comparing Hindi vs Hinglish vs English performance, or series/part-based Shorts.
