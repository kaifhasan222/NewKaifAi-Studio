# Growth plan (8 Nov 2026 audit)

Source: YouTube Studio dashboard + Ask Studio answer + web research. Facebook Business Suite par New Kaif Ai ka page nahi mila.

## Kya mila
- Channel 7 Nov ko bana. 12 Shorts, zyada tar 7 Nov ko 10 min mein. Studio ke hisaab se 117 views, stayed-to-watch ~29.7%.
- "0 views" abhi normal hai: channel 1-2 din ka hai, ek saath 9 upload se Shorts aapas mein compete karte hain.
- "Other notices" = YouTube ka automatic **AI label** (strike ya copyright nahi). Video details mein AI use "Yes, Set by YouTube".
- Metadata theek hai (QR video check kiya): description, 11 tags, Science & Technology, Hindi, not made for kids, playlist, comments on. Khali: "Title and description language".
- Video quality SD dikh rahi thi (HD process hona baaki ho sakta hai, 1080x1920 file se check karo).
- Facebook Business Suite mein sirf purana "LUCKY Gaming" page hai. New Kaif Ai ka page nahi.

## Fix (Ask Studio + research)
1. Upload: roz 1-2 Shorts, alag-alag time. Ek saath batch nahi. IST 1:00-2:30 PM ya 6:30-8:30 PM. Har Short ko 12-24 ghante data do.
2. Hook pehle 1-3 sec: intro/"aaj hum baat karenge" hatao. Sawal ya conflict se shuru. Pehle 0.5 sec mein motion ya bada text.
3. Title 50 character se chhota, curiosity pehle. Jaise "Phati Hui QR Code Kaise Scan Hoti Hai? 🤯 #Shorts".
4. Description 2-3 line + 3-5 hashtag. Tags par time mat lagao.
5. Text right side aur neeche 25% se door rakho (safe zone).
6. 2-3 hafte tak steady uploads, phir analytics se dekho kaun sa topic chala.
7. AI label: sach batao. Realistic AI scene ho to Yes rakhna sahi hai.

## Platform
- YouTube Shorts: abhi main platform (direct earning, discovery).
- Instagram Reels: watch time, completion aur sends sabse zaroori signal. Brand deals ke liye behtar. Wahi video cross-post karo.
- Facebook Reels: sabse kamzor evidence, monetization invite-only, payout figures blogs mein alag-alag. Pehle sirf test. Meta Professional Dashboard se verify karo.
- Facebook: pehle "New Kaif Ai" ka alag Page banao (Kaif khud). Phir Meta AI se poocho.
