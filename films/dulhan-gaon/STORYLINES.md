# Storylines

Source beats (Kaif): nayi dulhan bus se seema ke paas gaon jaati hai, akeli utarti hai, fauj ki aawajahai, khushi se khali raste chalti hai, sasural pahunchti hai. Extra screens isi safar ke hain. Reference video ka footage, script, music aur voice copy nahi.

## 1. Laal dupatta (picked)
Device: ek object aage badhta hai (laal dupatta). Form: object-to-object chain.
Viewer bus ki khidki se dupatta dekhta hai, phir wahi laal pairon tak, phir khali sadak, phir darwaze ki roshni.
Pehle 2s: dulhan bus mein, khidki par khet, line "Nayi dulhan".
Beats: bus → khidki → stop → bus jaati hai → seema par truck → khali raasta → phatak → darwaza khulta hai.
Look: gouache poster, dhool ochre, ek laal sirf dupatta aur title plate par.
Sound: garam acoustic bed, bus ke baad halka khulna, darwaze par ek hi lift.

## 2. Pairon ka rasta
Device: ek lamha uske din ka. Form: ek continuous take.
Camera sirf jutti aur sadak dekhta hai, bus ke step se aangan tak, bina cut.
Pehle 2s: jutti bus ke step par.
Beats: step → dhool → truck ka saya → phatak ki dehleez.
Look: niche camera, dhool aur laal kapda.
Sound: kadam aur doori ka bed.

## 3. Poster cuts
Device: type argument le jaati hai. Form: cut on the beat.
Har cut ek poora gouache poster, ek badi line.
Pehle 2s: poster "Nayi dulhan".
Beats: paanch poster se zyada, har ek naya scene.
Look: flat ink, bada type.
Sound: cuts beat par.
